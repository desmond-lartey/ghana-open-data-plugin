# Code of Conduct

Ghana Open Data is for everyone: students, researchers, journalists, public servants, community members and anyone curious about Ghana.

We expect everyone taking part to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new;
- discuss data and ideas, not people;
- credit others' work, and share only what you have the right to share;
- never post personal information about anyone.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and spam are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules.

To report a problem privately, open an issue asking for a private contact, without details, and a maintainer will reach you.
