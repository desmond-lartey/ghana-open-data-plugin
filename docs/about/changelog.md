# Changelog

## November 2026

- **The portal in use**: a public page with visits, downloads, the most used datasets and a world map of the countries people come from; each dataset page shows its own views, downloads and countries. Counted anonymously, by country only.
- **Places on the map**: the Map explorer draws Overture's points of interest (clinics, schools, cafés, shops, hotels, places of worship, transport and more) by group: rings when zoomed out, single points with names when zoomed in, and a filter by name or type.
- **Open in GIS workspace** now carries the explorer's map: the coloured districts or regions with every indicator as an attribute, and the places shown.
- **Map explorer**: compare Ghana's 260 districts and 16 regions on a map, table or ranking: buildings, roads, places, land cover (from Overture Maps, refreshed monthly), constituencies and polling stations.
- **Electoral geography**: the 276 parliamentary constituencies and 40,648 polling stations of the 2024 general elections, from the Electoral Commission's list, each linked to its district. Download them as CSV or query them in the Data Lab.
- **Languages**: the portal's menus and buttons in Asante Twi and Ewe (drafts; English stays the default).
- **Share data**: a Zotero-style form for sharing datasets, with DOI look-up, any file format and review by the team.
- **Accounts**: free accounts for downloads and the analysis tools; browsing stays open.
- **Fresher figures**: the IMF fills the years the World Bank has not published yet; Ghana's datasets on HDX are listed live.
- **GIS workspace**: the Ghana Open Data group on the Home tab, styled Ghana maps, and the switch from MODIS to VIIRS imagery.
- **Earth Engine**: draw fourteen datasets over Ghana with your own account, and download their values as GeoTIFF.
- **Desmond**: the portal's guide, on every page.
- **Usage figures** on the home page, counted without cookies.
