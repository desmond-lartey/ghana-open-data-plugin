# Privacy

## Browsing

The portal sets no cookies and has no adverts or third-party trackers. To show how it is used (visitors, page views, downloads and maps opened), each page sends one anonymous count. A random number kept in your browser tells a returning visitor from a new one; no name, email or IP address is stored, and visitors are estimated in a way that keeps no list of them. If your browser asks sites not to track you, nothing is counted. The count also records the country the visit comes from, as worked out by our hosting provider; we never see or keep the address itself, and countries with fewer than three visits are only shown as "other countries". The totals are public on [The portal in use](https://www.ghanaopendata.com/usage.html).

## Your account

We keep your name, email, the role and organisation you give, and whether you want occasional emails. We use them to run your account, to understand who uses Ghana's data, and, if you agree, to tell you about new data and features. We never sell them. Passwords are handled by our account service and are never visible to us.

## Shared data

When you share a dataset, we keep what you submit and the review notes. Once approved, the description, files and the name you choose to be credited with are public. Your email is never published.

## Earth Engine and Desmond

Earth Engine sign-in happens with Google, in your own account; the portal never sees your password or keeps your access. Desmond's conversation stays in your browser tab.

## Contact

To see, correct or delete your data, contact us through the [community page](https://github.com/desmond-lartey/ghana-open-data-plugin/issues) (for privacy matters, ask for a private contact rather than posting details).
