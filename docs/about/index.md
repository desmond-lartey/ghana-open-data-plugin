# About

Ghana Open Data is an independent, open data project for Ghana. It brings together open statistics, boundaries, satellite data and community datasets in one place, with tools to map and analyse them, for students, researchers, journalists, public servants and citizens.

## Principles

- **Open**: browsing is free and open to everyone; every dataset keeps its original licence.
- **Traceable**: every dataset links to its producer, with its date and credit line.
- **Fresh**: figures are read from their publishers, not copied once and left to age.
- **Careful**: community data is reviewed before it is published; personal data is never published.
- **Accessible**: the portal aims for WCAG 2.2 AA, and every chart has a table alternative.

## Not a government site

Ghana Open Data is not run by the Government of Ghana or the Ghana Statistical Service. For official statistics, the Ghana Statistical Service is the authority.
