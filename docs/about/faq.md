# FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes. Browsing is open to everyone, and accounts are free.

**Why do I need an account to download?**
Like Copernicus and NASA Earthdata, a free account tells us who uses Ghana's data and lets us tell you about changes. It takes about a minute.

**Is this official data?**
The portal republishes data from its producers, including official ones, with their credit lines. It is not a government site; for official statistics, the Ghana Statistical Service is the authority.

**Why is a figure several years old?**
Some indicators are measured by surveys and censuses that run every few years. See [Keeping data fresh](../data/freshness.md).

**Can I use the data in my thesis or article?**
Yes, under each dataset's licence. Cite the original source using the credit line on its page.

**Can I share my own data?**
Yes: see [Share data](../share/index.md). The review team checks every submission.

**Does the GIS workspace upload my files?**
No. Files you add are read on your computer.

**Earth Engine says I am not registered.**
Earth Engine needs your own registration and Cloud project; see [Earth Engine](../gis/earth-engine.md).

**I found a mistake.**
Please tell us on the [community page](https://github.com/desmond-lartey/ghana-open-data-plugin/issues).
