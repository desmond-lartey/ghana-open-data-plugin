# How to submit

Sharing works like adding an item to a reference manager such as Zotero.

1. **Sign in** (or create a free account), then open **Data › Share your data**.
2. **Choose the item type** on the left:

    | Type | For |
    |---|---|
    | Dataset | A table, a file or a collection of files |
    | Data from an article | The supplementary data of a published paper |
    | Report or statistical table | Tables from a report, bulletin or yearbook |
    | Map or GIS layer | Points, lines, areas or a raster |
    | Survey or census | Questionnaire results or microdata |
    | Satellite or aerial imagery | Images, drone surveys, derived maps |
    | Election results | By constituency, district or polling station |
    | Other | Anything else about Ghana |

3. **Add by identifier**: paste a DOI (from a journal, Zenodo, Dryad, Figshare…) and press **Add** to fill in the title, creators, date and publisher.
4. **Complete the information**: title, creators (authors, editors, contributors), date, a description, the licence, the themes, where in Ghana it covers and the period.
5. **Attach** the files, or add links to where the data is kept (a repository, a data portal, Google Drive…). Any format is welcome.
6. **Check** the list on the right: it shows what is still missing. Then **Submit**.

You can follow your submissions at the bottom of the page. A draft is kept in your browser while you work.

!!! tip "Only share what you may share"
    Make sure you have the right to share the data, and give its licence. If you are not sure which licence applies, say so in the description and we will help. Never share personal data about identifiable people.
