# How we review

Every submission is checked by the Ghana Open Data review team before it appears anywhere.

## What we check

- **Licence**: that the data may be shared, and under which terms.
- **Source**: that the authors and the original publication are correctly credited, and the DOI or link works.
- **Files**: that they open, match the description, and contain no personal data about identifiable people.
- **Description**: that a reader can tell what the data is, where and when it covers, and how it was made.

## Decisions

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Waiting for review | Received; the team has not looked at it yet |
| Changes requested | The team needs something from you; the note says what. Edit and resubmit |
| Approved | Published on the portal and in the [community catalogue](catalogue.md) |
| Not accepted | It does not fit the portal; the note explains why |

You see the decision and the team's note under **Your submissions** on the Share data page.

## Corrections and withdrawals

To correct or withdraw a published dataset, open an issue on the [community page](https://github.com/desmond-lartey/ghana-open-data-plugin/issues) or contact us through the portal.
