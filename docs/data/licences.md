# Licences and citing

Every dataset keeps the licence of its producer. Its page shows the licence and the exact credit line to use, and the **Cite** tab gives a ready-made reference.

| Data | Licence | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Boundaries | CC BY 3.0 IGO | OCHA Ghana Common Operational Dataset (COD-AB) |
| National indicators | CC BY 4.0 | World Bank, World Development Indicators; IMF World Economic Outlook |
| NASA imagery | Public domain | NASA EOSDIS GIBS and the named satellite |
| Earth Engine datasets | Each dataset's own terms | The dataset's producer, via Google Earth Engine |
| Community datasets | As given by the contributor | The authors and the original publication |

## Citing the portal

> Ghana Open Data (2026). *Ghana Open Data portal*. https://www.ghanaopendata.com

Cite the original dataset as well, using the credit line on its page.
