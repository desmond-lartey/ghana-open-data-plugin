# Data & sources

Ghana Open Data republishes open data from its original producers, with their licences and credit lines, and brings in fresh figures from the bodies that publish them.

| Source | What it provides | How it reaches the portal |
|---|---|---|
| OCHA Common Operational Dataset | National, regional and district boundaries, capitals, p-codes | Republished as files |
| World Bank, World Development Indicators | National indicators, comparison countries | Live, cached for a day |
| IMF World Economic Outlook | Recent years and current-year estimates for economic indicators | Live, filling years the World Bank lacks |
| NASA GIBS | Daily and multi-day satellite imagery | Live map tiles |
| Google Earth Engine | Land cover, elevation, rainfall, population and more | On request, with your own account |
| Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) | Ghana's datasets from UN agencies and NGOs | Listed live from HDX's catalogue |
| The community | Data from articles, surveys, reports and maps | Shared and reviewed on the portal |

See [Keeping data fresh](freshness.md) for how figures stay up to date, and [Licences and citing](licences.md) for how to credit them.
