# Keeping data fresh

The portal reads its figures from the publishers when you look at them, rather than keeping copies that age.

## National indicators

- **World Bank** World Development Indicators are the backbone: long, comparable series for every country. They lag a year or more.
- **IMF** World Economic Outlook figures (updated each April and October) fill the years the World Bank has not published yet for GDP, growth, inflation, GDP per person, population and unemployment. The current year is an IMF estimate and is labelled as one; forecasts are never shown as the latest value.

## Satellite imagery

NASA's imagery is requested for the newest day it has published. Where a satellite stops (MODIS on Terra and Aqua is being wound down in 2026–27), the portal moves to its successor (VIIRS) automatically.

## Datasets from other portals

Ghana's datasets on the Humanitarian Data Exchange are listed live, newest first, so new uploads by UN agencies and NGOs appear without anyone copying them.

## Why some figures are old

Indicators measured by national surveys and censuses (literacy, poverty, school attendance) are updated only when a new survey is published, sometimes every five years or more. The portal says when the latest figure is three or more years old and points to the Ghana Statistical Service.
